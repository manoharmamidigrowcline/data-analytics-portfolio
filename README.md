# 📊 Data Analytics Portfolio

### Six Industry-Oriented Data Analytics Projects

Welcome to the **Data Analytics Portfolio**, a collection of six practical, business-oriented projects designed to demonstrate end-to-end data analytics skills using real-world datasets and industry-relevant tools.

This repository showcases the complete analytics lifecycle — from data collection, cleaning, transformation, and exploratory data analysis to SQL querying, interactive dashboard development, and business insights.

The objective is to transform raw data into meaningful insights that support better business decisions.

# 📁 Project Details

## 1. Retail Sales & Revenue Analytics

**Domain:** Retail & Business Intelligence

### Overview

Analyze retail sales data to understand revenue performance, product profitability, customer purchasing behavior, and regional sales trends.

### Key Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Evaluate regional and customer-wise sales.
- Track monthly and yearly revenue growth.
- Identify profitable and underperforming products.

### Key KPIs

- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Profit Margin
- Monthly Sales Growth

### Technologies

Excel | SQL | Python | Pandas | Power BI

### Expected Deliverables

- Cleaned retail dataset.
- SQL business analysis queries.
- Python exploratory data analysis.
- Interactive Power BI sales dashboard.
- Business insights and recommendations.

# 📈 Project Workflow

Each project follows a structured analytics workflow:

1. **Business Understanding** – Define the problem and business objectives.
2. **Data Collection** – Identify and acquire relevant datasets.
3. **Data Cleaning** – Handle missing values, duplicates, and inconsistencies.
4. **Data Exploration** – Discover patterns, trends, and relationships.
5. **SQL Analysis** – Perform queries to extract business insights.
6. **Python Analysis** – Conduct EDA and statistical analysis.
7. **Dashboard Development** – Build interactive Power BI reports.
8. **Insights & Recommendations** – Translate findings into actionable business decisions.
